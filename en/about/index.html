About United Flowers

From the sunflower to the Saudi kitchen.

United Flowers was founded in Jeddah to make vegetable oils and fats that families and bakers can trust.

From our factory in Jeddah Industrial City we refine, fill and pack a full range of cooking oils, ghee and margarine, sold under our own brands and made to order as private label. Our factory is equipped with modern technology and run by an experienced team that follows all health regulations and strict quality standards. Today we are growing into processed cheese and sauces.
